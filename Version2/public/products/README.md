# Product photos

Upload the three product image files into this folder:

1. `product1.png` — Matte Black
2. `product2.png` — Brushed Gold
3. `product3.png` — Polished Chrome

Recommended: square or portrait composition, at least 1200 px on the longest side, with the product centered and enough breathing room around it. Keep the exact lowercase filenames. These images are served at `/products/product1.png`, `/products/product2.png`, and `/products/product3.png`.
